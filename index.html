Chrome Extension

Pintspiration

Capture a full-page screenshot of any website and save it straight to a Pinterest board. No downloading, no re-uploading.

How it works

  1. 1

    Capture

    Click the toolbar icon. The whole page is captured as one tall image.

  2. 2

    Choose

    Pick one of your boards and adjust the title.

  3. 3

    Save

    Press Save. The Pin links back to the original page.

  • Captures only when you click. Nothing runs in the background.
  • Sends data only to Pinterest, and only when you press Save.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
