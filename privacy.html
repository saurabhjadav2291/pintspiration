Pinspiration

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Pinspiration is a personal Chrome extension that captures a full-page screenshot of the website you are viewing and, when you choose, saves it as a Pin to one of your own Pinterest boards.

What data is used

  • Screenshots and page details. When you click the extension and press Capture, it takes a screenshot of the current tab and reads that page's title and URL. This only happens after you click.
  • Pinterest connection. When you connect your Pinterest account, Pinterest gives the extension an access token. The extension never sees your Pinterest password.
  • Your account and boards. The extension reads your Pinterest username and board list so you can choose where to save.

Where data goes

  • Screenshots, titles, descriptions and links are sent only to Pinterest, and only when you press Save.
  • A small sign-in service exchanges the Pinterest authorization code for tokens. It receives only that code or a refresh token and does not store anything.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. There is no analytics or tracking, and no browsing history is collected.

Storage

  • Tokens, your username and preferences are stored locally in your browser (Chrome extension storage), on your device only.
  • Screenshots are kept locally until they are saved or discarded, then deleted.

Your choices

  • Press Disconnect in the extension to delete the stored tokens.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time in your Pinterest account settings (Security → Apps).
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all of its local data.
